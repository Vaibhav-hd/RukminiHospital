## 🎨 Customization Guide

### Change Colors
Open `index.html` and replace:
- `#0b7a5c` → Your primary color
- `#0f4c81` → Your secondary color

### Change Fonts
Find in `<style>`:
```css
font-family: 'Segoe UI', Tahoma, Geneva, Verdana, sans-serif;
```
Replace with your preferred font

### Add/Remove Services
Find services section and duplicate/remove cards:
```html
<div class="card">
    <div class="card-icon"><i class="fas fa-icon"></i></div>
    <h3>Service Name</h3>
    <p>Description</p>
</div>
```

### Update Images
1. Replace `logo.png` with your logo
2. Replace `doctor.jpg` with doctor's photo
3. Keep same filenames
